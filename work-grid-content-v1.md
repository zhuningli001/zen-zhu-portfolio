# Work Grid Content v1 — Ningli Zhu Portfolio

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the homepage work grid content for Ningli Zhu's personal / studio website.

The grid should work as a visual index: fast to scan, visually rich, and structured enough to prove senior-level design judgment.

The goal is not to show every project. The goal is to make visitors think:

> She can work across AI products, complex systems, design knowledge, brand/web experiences and cultural projects — with structure, taste and delivery.

## 2. Grid Strategy

### Recommended First Grid

Use 8 primary cards:

1. YIOne / 亿万 APP
2. Momcozy AI Component & Module Knowledge System
3. Momcozy Website Redesign & Upgrade
4. CXD AI Product Marketing Design Platform
5. Factualist
6. Jing Qiong Er
7. Cynergy
8. ECA International Cultural Creative Award

### Layout Weight

Recommended visual hierarchy:

```text
[Large]  YIOne
[Large]  Momcozy AI Knowledge System
[Medium] Momcozy Website Redesign
[Medium] CXD AI Design Platform
[Small]  Factualist
[Small]  Jing Qiong Er
[Small]  Cynergy
[Small]  ECA
```

Reason:

- The first four cards establish the new AI-native / design technologist direction.
- The last four cards prove cultural, brand, web, and visual delivery range.
- This avoids the old "I do many styles" feeling and creates a clearer professional signal.
